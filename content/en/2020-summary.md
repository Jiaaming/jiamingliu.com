# March

The most important thing was probably the city-wide mock exam.

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Jiaaming/blogImage/main/pic/!\[我和猫猫]\(\).png)

Me and the cat.
I remember this photo very clearly — it was taken before the math exam.

That time I finished the math paper almost **an hour before the exam ended** (except the insane final problem).
I confidently told my mom:

> “You can collect the paper. This test is ridiculously easy. No way I score below 140.”

(I was really stupid, honestly.)

My mom kept telling me:

> “Check your answers!”

And guess what — I messed up the multiple choice and fill-in-the-blank probability questions…

That mock exam score was… not pretty.

After the exam, until the next mock exam in Xicheng District, I kept comforting myself every day:

> “Shanghai University isn’t bad either!”

Not long after that something pretty major happened — **the Gaokao was postponed**.

I remember it very clearly: **March 31st, around 11:30 a.m., during history class.**
Someone suddenly typed in the meeting chat that the Gaokao had been delayed.

There were originally **68 days left until the exam** — just over two months. I was basically in despair and had almost given up.

Suddenly it became **97 days**.

I instantly went:

> WOOHOO!!!

No more browsing Taobao, no more magic tricks, no more messing with cameras.
It suddenly felt like I still had a chance.

Thank you, Examination Authority, for giving me another chance to start over.

Three years of luck finally kicked in haha.

# June

The last days of student life.

![Birthday cake. Smelled amazing. The cat clearly wanted some too.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Jiaaming/blogImage/main/pic/20251130200442.png)

Originally my birthday was supposed to coincide with **the first day of Gaokao**.

For many years I had imagined that day:
**Turning 18 + Gaokao on the same day.**

Pretty dramatic.

But in the end, things changed and I missed that coincidence.

Then on **June 17th**, the **second wave of the pandemic** suddenly hit.

A sudden graduation ceremony announcement exploded everyone's WeChat Moments.

![JoJo pose — my favorite photo. Men's happiness is really this simple.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Jiaaming/blogImage/main/pic/20251130200513.png)

Even though it was only half a year, it somehow felt like a very long time.

Back home again, suffering through online classes with a painful expression.

I **really hate online classes**.
They always give me the feeling that I learned nothing.

Self-studying and grinding through problems always felt much better.

About a week later we heard online classes would last until early July.

My mentality completely collapsed.

That night I talked to my homeroom teacher until **10:30 PM**.

The only thing I clearly remember saying was:

> “If… if my Gaokao results end up being disappointing…
> I will regret that period of time for the rest of my life.”

Looking back now, I feel both brave and embarrassingly dramatic.

But I’m truly, truly grateful that **Teacher Li supported my decision.**

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Jiaaming/blogImage/main/pic/20251130200538.png)
